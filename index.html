Everywhere

Nyxen in the tray

The Nyxen icon sits by the clock. Click it to open Nyxen; right-click it and the everyday things are one click away - without opening anything.

  • The song playing on your PC: pause, next, previous
  • Your status, muting or leaving the call, and the games you played lately
  • A screenshot, Messages, the live map and the Space
  • Close the window and Nyxen stays here, like Discord - messages and calls still reach you
  • Start with Windows if you like, and Quit when you want it gone
Minecraft

Your world, in real 3D blocks

Flip the live map's 2D | 3D switch (or press M) and your world turns into cubes - read straight from the save on your PC, with the trees, water, snow and the houses you built. Your friends walk around on it and your waypoints glow as beacons.

  • Smooth: built in the background, only what is on screen is drawn, and nothing at all while it sits still
  • Switching between 2D and 3D is instant, and Nyxen remembers the one you like
  • Textures uses your own game's block pictures, Clean draws it like a model
  • Angled, from the top or close to the ground - drag to turn, scroll to zoom
  • Full screen with your hotbar, health, the call and the time of day
  • Explore a world in 3D with the game closed - Overworld, Nether and End
Beta

Nyxen Space: hang out in 3D

A tab in Social: a white grid floor under a soft grey sky - or a night one - where you and your friends walk around as shiny figures with a floating head, shoulders, arms and legs, dressed the way you like.

  • Your look: any colour in chrome, glossy, jelly, two-tone or oil-slick, height, build, head and a hat
  • Talk: a real voice call, quieter the further away people stand - a green mic floats over whoever is talking
  • Say something and it pops up in a blue bubble over your head
  • DJ decks: paste any song link and everyone there hears it, in time - louder near the decks
  • Sit on the couch and watch whoever shares their screen on the TV - or on a cinema screen
  • Reactions: a heart, a laugh, fire, a party... pop up over your head for everyone (keys 1-8)
  • A pet that trots after you - a cat, a dog, a bunny, a duck, a little dragon or a glow orb
  • Photo (P): what you see, saved with your screenshots
  • Make your own spaces and invite friends - they get a card in Messages and walk straight in
Nyxen Space

Quick build: drag an area, pick what goes there

Pick Quick build, drag over the ground and choose - a house, a shop, an office tower, apartments, a park, a plaza, parking or a road. It is built to fit the area you dragged, facing you. The street above took seven drags.

  • Ready-made pieces: brick and white walls, walls with windows or a door, glass, fences, stairs, a roof and a floor - placed in one click
  • Walls and rooms in one drag, floors in one drag, and Clear it empties an area
  • A bigger hotbar with names under every slot - click a slot again for every piece and colour
  • Every tool in one row: Place, Quick build, Wall, Room, Floor, Move, Paint, Pick, Erase, Undo and Redo
  • A top view (Tab) to plan a whole street, then walk it
  • See what you are about to build before you click - and Move to rearrange anything later
Nyxen Space

Your view, your way

See the Space through your own eyes, fill the whole screen with it, and pick day or night - for you, without changing it for anyone else.

  • First person: one button, F5, or scroll all the way in - scroll out to see yourself again
  • Full screen: the Space takes the whole monitor, Esc brings Nyxen back
  • Dark or White: one button flips the Space between a night sky and a bright grey one
  • Left click builds, right click removes, the middle button picks up what you point at
Assistant

A voice that sounds like a person

One click in Settings and the assistant stops sounding like a robot. The natural voice runs entirely on your PC - no key to paste, and nothing you say is sent anywhere.

  • Ten voices: Heart, Bella, Nicole, Michael, Fenrir, Puck, Emma, George - and Spanish
  • Starts talking while it is still thinking of the next sentence
  • One download, about 120 MB, and it stays out of your game's way
Friends

Social, with a real home

See who is online, what they are playing and what they are listening to - with the song's cover, straight from Spotify or Apple Music, the moment it changes.

  • Your game and your song at the top, the feed below
  • Live rooms to jump into, and people you may know
  • Message or call anyone from the list in one click
Look

Ten layouts, not just colours

Interface styles move the navigation itself: a sidebar, a slim rail, a top bar, floating glass, a Mac-style dock, Zen, Couch for the TV - or Hub, with your friends and your music in a column beside everything.

What is in it

Everything it does

Every item here is a feature that exists in the build on this page. Nothing on this list is planned, coming soon, or a mock-up.

New

Nyxen Space (beta)

A 3D place for friends: chrome figures, voice that fades with distance and a mic over whoever talks, DJ decks, a couch, a TV and a cinema screen, quick build, first person, full screen, dark or white, reactions, pets and photos.

New

Themes, with a tab of their own

Every theme as a big preview in its own colours - hover one and the whole app wears it, click to keep it and it spreads out from where you clicked. Search, categories, Surprise me, and the layouts side by side.

New

What should I play?

One click and Nyxen picks from your installed games - leaning towards the ones you nearly finished or have not touched in a while. Play it, roll another, or invite friends. The Library also shows Almost done and Never played, with your real Steam play times.

New

Invites that start the game

Invite friends to play any Steam game, or to your space, from the Library or the Space. They get a card in Messages with the game's art - one click on Play and Steam starts it (or offers to install it).

New

Minecraft in 3D blocks

The live map as real cubes, from your world's save - with your friends, waypoints and the time of day. Works with the game closed too.

New

A natural voice

The assistant speaks like a person, on your PC, with no key. Ten voices to pick from.

Mods

Four mod sites, one search box

Thunderstore, Modrinth, GameBanana and the Steam Workshop, searched together and interleaved so one site's catalogue does not bury the other's.

  • Tick several mods, install them in one go
  • Checks each build against your loader before downloading
  • Turn a mod off without deleting it
  • Share a mod list with friends; their copy fetches the files
  • Brings Workshop items into games that do not read that folder
Minecraft

Whatever launcher you already use

Reads the official launcher, Prism, MultiMC, the Modrinth app, CurseForge, ATLauncher, GDLauncher and XMCL, wherever they already are. Nothing extra to install.

  • Mods, shaders, resource packs and data packs
  • A brand new instance with no mods at all
  • Export a .mrpack to send someone
  • See what is installed, per instance, and toggle it
  • Server list with live pings; recommendations for your pack
Minecraft

Your skin, in 3D

A real model you turn with the mouse, drawn without any 3D library so it works offline. Look up any player's skin by name, and wear one when you sign in to Minecraft.

Retro

RetroArch and RetroAchievements

Finds RetroArch by itself and identifies your ROMs by hash against 141 systems. No key to paste, and it works offline after the first lookup.

  • What you are playing, its platform and its player count
  • Cheats, from RetroArch's folder or libretro's 28,000 files
  • Your achievement progress, using the login RetroArch stores
  • A shelf of box art for everything it recognised
Achievements

Your collection, and what is close

Everything you have earned across your library, sorted by how near you are to finishing it.

  • Rarest achievements, and rarity for the game you are in
  • Nearly there, and the graveyard of what you abandoned
  • Per-game completion and a running total
Friends

Friends, with no company in the middle

Profiles, direct messages, calls and screen sharing between your own installs. The server is one small file you can run yourself.

  • Profile with what you are playing and its art
  • Groups, challenges and a leaderboard
  • Friends join with an invite link
Assistant

An assistant that can see the game

Ask about the game you are in. It can read the screen when you ask it to, keep notes between sessions, and coach you through a run.

Toolkit

The small things, in the same window

  • Custom crosshair overlay
  • Screenshots, with a gallery
  • Notes that stay with the game
  • Audio mixer and media controls
  • Calculator, colour picker, randomizer
  • Session timers and input statistics

58 themes, eighteen of them alive

Inside the app the backgrounds move — auroras, a synthwave sunset, a turning galaxy, rain with lightning, cherry blossom and more. All of it stops when the window is not in front, so it costs nothing while you are in a game.

Before you install

The awkward parts, up front rather than afterwards.

It is in early access Nyxen is 1.0 Early Access: everything on this page works today, and new builds arrive through the app on their own. The Space is still marked beta.
Windows only Windows 10 and 11. There is no macOS or Linux build.
Not code-signed SmartScreen will warn you the first time, as it does for any installer without a paid certificate. The SHA-256 below lets you check the file instead.
You need a Nyxen account It is free and you make it in the app in a few seconds. It keeps your friends, messages, the Space and your settings.
Some features use logins you already have A Minecraft skin change needs a Microsoft sign-in. Retro progress reads the RetroAchievements login RetroArch stores, and only after you switch that on.
The assistant works without a key Nyxen AI answers typed questions with nothing to set up. Reading your screen needs Ollama on your own PC, and you can switch the assistant off in Settings.
Nothing is uploaded Library, screenshots, notes and statistics stay on your PC. Mod downloads come straight from the mod sites.
Uninstalls cleanly Per-user install, an ordinary Add / Remove Programs entry, and your settings survive updates.
